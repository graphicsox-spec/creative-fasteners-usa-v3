# Homepage Banner Image Shortlist

Status: Approved and implemented in the Step 2 homepage slider

Prepared: 3 October 2026

## Creative direction

- One wide image for each of six service industries.
- Industrial, engineering, maintenance, and equipment-led imagery rather than action or combat scenes.
- Neutral charcoal, steel, blue, and grey tones that can be unified with a subtle color grade.
- Sufficient negative space on the left for headline, supporting copy, and a quote CTA.
- Avoid visible third-party branding, identifiable insignia where practical, and imagery that could imply endorsement.
- Public-facing label recommendation: **Firearms** rather than **Gun**.
- Public-facing label recommendation: **Military & Defense** rather than **Military** alone.

## Recommended primary set

### 1. Firearms

**Primary:** [Gunsmith / AR-15 workshop image — Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/photos/gun-gunsmith-ar-15-rifle-ar15-m16-5319040/)

- 3000 × 2005 source image; suitable for a wide crop.
- Clearly communicates firearms and gunsmithing without showing active use.
- Use a darker steel color grade and crop toward mechanical detail.

**Backup:** [Industrial gunsmith workbench — Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-workbench-filled-with-a-variety-of-tools-3aG08v591o0)

- Workshop/manufacturing mood fits the company's precision positioning.
- Less explicit and therefore safer for a professional industrial homepage.

### 2. Oil & Gas

**Primary:** [Large refinery with pipes and towers — Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-photo-of-an-industrial-business-4883682/)

- 5616 × 3744, 3:2 landscape source.
- Strong wide composition and good room for a headline overlay.
- Steel, grey, and blue palette already aligns with the proposed redesign.

**Backup:** [Oil refinery plant with intricate pipelines — Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/oil-refinery-plant-15970032/)

- More detail-led industrial composition.
- Useful if the primary crop leaves insufficient text space.

### 3. Aerospace

**Primary:** [Close-up of a jet-engine turbine — Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/close-up-of-a-jet-engines-intricate-turbine-eeCmlu95MmI)

- Precision-mechanical subject directly supports the fastener story.
- Strong visual focal point with natural charcoal and metallic tones.
- Prefer over a generic airplane-in-flight image.

**Backup:** [Commercial airplane in a maintenance hangar — Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/commercial-airplane-in-maintenance-hangar-37616793/)

- Wide industrial setting and immediate aerospace recognition.
- Maintenance context is more relevant than travel imagery.

### 4. Commercial / Industrial

**Primary:** [Modern CNC machinery in a factory — Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/modern-industrial-machinery-in-a-factory-setting-38804515/)

- Communicates precision engineering and modern manufacturing.
- Strong match for Creative Fasteners' commercial-production audience.

**Backup:** [Wide factory interior with conveyor systems — Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/industrial-factory-interior-with-conveyor-34718930/)

- Wide composition is well suited to a homepage banner.
- Useful as a broader commercial-manufacturing image.

### 5. Law Enforcement

**Primary:** [Close-up of police emergency lights — Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/vehicle-blue-emergency-light-turned-on-532001/)

- Brand-neutral close-up with no identifiable officer or department endorsement.
- Blue-light color naturally connects with the site's accent color.
- Crop tightly enough to avoid unnecessary vehicle branding.

**Backup:** [Police vehicle with lights in an urban setting — Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-police-car-on-the-road-12777394/)

- Broader context and a clear law-enforcement signal.
- Darker night setting will need a restrained overlay to remain consistent with the lighter redesign.

### 6. Military & Defense

**Primary:** [Military helicopter maintenance — Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/black-chopper-2059638/)

- Engineering and maintenance-led rather than combat-led.
- Communicates defense capability without an aggressive action scene.

**Backup:** [Military aircraft at an airbase with personnel — Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/military-aircraft-at-airbase-with-personnel-28553438/)

- Strong wide composition and recognizable defense setting.
- Use only with a crop that minimizes insignia and avoids implying endorsement.

- [Pixabay license summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/): free use and modification without required attribution, subject to restrictions around recognizable brands, trademarks, people, misleading use, and standalone redistribution.

## Implementation preparation after approval

1. Re-check the selected source page and license immediately before download.
2. Download the highest-resolution original from the source page.
3. Preserve a source record containing page URL, photographer, download date, and license URL.
4. Crop all six images to a consistent desktop banner ratio while protecting mobile focal points.
5. Export optimized WebP and responsive fallback sizes.
6. Apply one consistent charcoal/steel color treatment across the set.
