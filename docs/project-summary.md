## 2. Brand Positioning & Marketing Angle
The website must project maximum credibility, engineering precision, and military-grade quality assurance. Key themes to highlight include:
* **Zero-Tolerance Quality:** Focus on critical aerospace/defense applications (fighter jets, spacecraft, missiles).
* **Process Router & Inspection Control:** Emphasize step-by-step dimensional verification and process routers governing each production stage.
* **Compliance Standards:** Prominently display US Military and Aerospace compliance indicators (AS9100 / ISO 9001 standards).

---

## 3. Scope of Deliverables

### A. Brand Identity
* **Logo Design:** Complete creation of a modern, high-tech industrial logo for Creative Fasteners USA.

### B. Single-Page Website Features
1. **Hero Section:**
   * High-impact layout featuring an **animated video slider** (FWSI.net design style).
   * Strong value proposition headline and quick RFQ call-to-action.
2. **Manufacturing Process Breakdown:**
   * Hot and Cold Forming
   * Pointers & Threaders
   * Precision Drilling
   * Heat Treating & Passivation
   * Plating (including specialized Titanium Plating)
3. **Quality & Compliance Section:**
   * Quality control workflow, in-process router inspection steps, and dimensional precision benchmarks.
   * Compliance icons/badges for defense and aerospace readiness.
4. **Lead Conversion / RFQ Funnel:**
   * Prominent "Click through to place an order" / Request For Quote (RFQ) section.
